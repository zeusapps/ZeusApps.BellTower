2. Claude fork (QA-002). Implemented in `IdentityHistoryReader` (adapter) plus `IdentityInheritance` (Core).
   - Parent verified from the fork transcript's own `forkedFrom.sessionId` on records whose `sessionId` is the fork's (fixture `claude-sdk-0.3.309-fork.jsonl`). The reader's relevance pre-filter now also admits `forkedFrom` lines.
