## Assessment and limitations

All assigned rechecks pass at the fixture and built-binary level, build, two full test runs (666/666, no flake), node tests and verify-only format pass, and the review identity is unchanged. I do not close ledger findings.

Clock decision (no tolerance): the floor is the tray's detection time of the switch, compared with provider timestamps from the same machine. This is the conservative direction: pre-switch work can never be re-selected. The cost is that a history record stamped between the actual `git switch` and the tray noticing it is also rejected from history recovery; I simulated this (work stamped about 2 s before the recorded floor was rejected). Live hook evidence is not floor-filtered, so ordinary post-switch work still associates through hooks, and later post-switch work from history is accepted. I judge this acceptable for the final round and record it as a known trade-off, not a finding. A backward clock adjustment cannot lower the floor (it only moves forward).
