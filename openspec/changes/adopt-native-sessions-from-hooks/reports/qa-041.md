## Findings

### QA-4100: The pump's "survivor writes again after a restore" behavior has no test

- Severity: warning
- Related task / requirement: 4.3; ANALYSIS-3301; implementation-047 "After a restore, ..."
- Location: `src/BellTower/Hosts/TerminalTitlePump.cs:175-179` (tests: `tests/BellTower.Tests/Hosts/NativePresentationTests.cs`)
- Expected / actual: new behavior added by DEV-047 should be covered. Actual: replacing the same-shell receipt-forgetting condition with `false` leaves all 73 presentation, mixed-launch and title tests green, so nothing asserts that a presented session in the same shell writes again after another session's restore. Without that, a survivor would keep its receipt, never rewrite, and the shell would stay on the prior title with no Bell Tower title or palette. The failure-and-retry branch of `Restore` (record kept, 30 s retry) is likewise unexecuted by any test.
- Reproduction: change `item.Value.Desired?.Shell == record.Shell` to `false` in `TerminalTitlePump.RestoreUnpresentedAsync`, then run the presentation tests (all pass); restore the file.
- Evidence: mutant survived (see Checks table); restored with matching hash.
- Recommended correction: add a pump test with two presented sessions in one shell where the record owner ends (or the owner alone becomes unpresented): assert a restore for the owner and then a fresh write for the survivor on the next refresh; add a case where the restore fails and the record plus retry are kept.
- Relationship: new

### QA-4101: Two non-Codex tests also fail under concurrent full-suite load

- Severity: suggestion
- Related task / requirement: test reliability; related to deferred QA-4001
- Location: `tests/BellTower.Tests/Tray/ReminderPlaybackTests.cs` (`ActualWindowsSourceAllowsHealthyReminderThroughQueue`), `tests/BellTower.Tests/Tray/AudioQueueTests.cs` (`ResolvedReminderDuringAggregateToneCancelsAggregateSpeech`)

- Recommended correction: orchestrator to decide whether to fold into deferred QA-4001; no change needed for this slice.
- Relationship: related to QA-4001 (deferred)
