## Assessment and limitations

- **Verdict:** DEV-067 implements ANALYSIS-5600 as proposed. There is one Core rule with a Codex adapter fact and no per-provider policy in Core. The owned runtime and Claude paths are unchanged, a renamed flag fails closed, and the hook budget is not increased. The matrix, doctor and documentation describe the new boundary honestly for both clients.
- **ANALYSIS-5601:**
  - Keeping the transport gate satisfies the finding. A transport that called the narrower predicate would refuse non-managed `app-server` peers it accepts today, and the finding's risk section ruled that out.
  - Decision for the orchestrator: reconcile the planning wording, since design decision 7 (revision) and task 4.12 say the transport "uses the same predicate". Suggested text: "the transport keeps its `app-server` gate, of which the managed-daemon predicate is a subset by construction (pinned by tests)". The alternative is to ask the user whether the transport should instead call the predicate and accept being stricter.
- **Not performed:**
  - Build, tests and format checks: owned by QA-062.
  - Live acceptance on a real orphaned or updater-restarted backend: task 4.12 / 6.1, which needs the next dev build.
  - Hook-time measurements: the budget statement is a source observation.
- **Orphan helper:** isolation holds. As a failure-path observation only, a launcher that did not exit within 20 s would not be killed by the `finally`. This is not raised as a finding under recheck rules.
