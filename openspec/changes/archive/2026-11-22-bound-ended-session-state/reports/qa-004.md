## Findings

### QA-300: An apostrophe that ends a directory name (plural possessive) still leaks the rest of the path

- Severity: warning (content safety; low likelihood)
- Related task / requirement: 2.1; design decision 4 ("every path replaced, including a path that contains an apostrophe"); finding ANALYSIS-200
- Location: `src/BellTower/IO/RefreshHealth.cs:183` (`PathTail`), `:203` (`Quoted`), `:207` (`PathPattern`) at 5e95554 plus working tree
- Expected / actual: a path whose directory name ends in an apostrophe followed by a space, such as `D:\Clients\Smiths' Project\notes\plan.md`, should be fully masked. An apostrophe followed by whitespace is treated as the closing quote (quoted case) or ends the path (unquoted case), so the remainder reaches `bell-tower.log`, `refresh-health.json` and doctor.
- Reproduction: `RefreshHealth.Describe(new IOException(input), @"D:\data\x", @"C:\Users\Pat", "Pat")` with these inputs, synthetic names only:
  - `Could not find file 'D:\Clients\Smiths' Project\notes\plan.md'.` gives `Could not find file '<path>' Project\notes\plan.md'.`
  - `Failed D:\Clients\Smiths' Project\notes\plan.md now` gives `Failed <path>' Project\notes\plan.md now`
  - `Failed C:\Users\Pat\Smiths' Project\notes\plan.md now` (profile subfolder, unquoted) gives `Failed <path>' Project\notes\plan.md now`
  - `Failed '/srv/Smiths' stuff/y.txt' now` gives `Failed '<path>' stuff/y.txt' now`
  - `'D:\x\Rock 'n' Roll\f.txt' bad` gives `'<path>' Roll\f.txt' bad`
  - `'D:\a\Bob'\f' end` gives `'<path>'\f' end`
- Evidence: scratch probe against the built `bell-tower.dll` at the identity above. The developer's four ANALYSIS-200 cases and two-quoted-paths case pass; these variants are the same defect (an apostrophe inside a path leaks its tail) through the closing-apostrophe rule that the fix introduced. The quoted case is ambiguous by nature, so a rule of "close only when whitespace follows" cannot distinguish `Smiths' Project\...'` from a real close.
- Recommended correction: when a candidate closing apostrophe is followed by whitespace, keep reading if the text up to the next whitespace token or next apostrophe still contains a path separator (`\` or `/`); apply the same continuation to the unquoted rules. When unsure, mask more rather than less (over-masking is not a defect here). Add the cases above to `RefreshHealthTests` asserting no backslash, slash or name fragment after the mask.
- Relationship: residual of ANALYSIS-200 (the fix is incomplete for this form)

### QA-301: Recovery lines and recovery records are outside the stated bound once reasons change between failures

- Severity: warning (low likelihood; bounded)
- Related task / requirement: 2.1; design decision 4 ("any mix of failures and successes stays within the same bound as a continuous failure"); `docs/architecture.md` tray logging paragraph ("about nine lines per 10 minutes whatever the reasons"); finding ANALYSIS-201
- Location: `src/BellTower/IO/RefreshHealth.cs:104-107` (recovery line written for every logged window) and `:76-82` (record written when a logged failure follows a recovery)
- Expected / actual: ANALYSIS-201 is fixed for a repeating reason (2 lines and 2 record writes per 10 minutes under strict alternation, down from one per tick). With a new reason on each failure, or 12 rotating reasons, alternating with successes, each of the 8 remembered reasons logs a failure line and then a recovery line, so output is 17 lines and 16 atomic record writes per 10 minutes, not the nine lines the design and docs state. The bound is constant and does not grow with ticks, so the spec's "without one entry per tick" holds.
- Reproduction: fake clock, `RefreshHealth(paths, clock)`, 2,400 ticks of 250 ms; on even ticks `Failed(new IOException("r" + i))`, on odd ticks `Succeeded()`; count lines in `bell-tower.log` and writes of `refresh-health.json`.
- Evidence: probe output `alt-newreason: lines in 10min=17 recordWrites~16`; same for 12 rotating reasons.
- Recommended correction: either state the bound as "about 17 lines per 10 minutes (up to 9 failure lines and 8 recovery lines)" in design decision 4 and `docs/architecture.md`, or limit recovery lines and recovery/failure record writes to once per interval. Either choice needs a test with changing reasons plus successes.
- Relationship: residual of ANALYSIS-201; raised as a design/behavior mismatch, not a per-tick defect. The orchestrator decides whether it blocks.
