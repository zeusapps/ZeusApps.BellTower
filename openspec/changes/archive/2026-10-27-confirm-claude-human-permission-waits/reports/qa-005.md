## Assessment and limitations

The Release build and scoped format verification pass. The full suite is 974/976 because two hook latency assertions failed during the broad run; both pass in one isolated same-bits rerun, so the environmental cause is plausible but not proven. Keep the full-suite failure visible.

Implementation 004 fixes the initial stale-response race and the A→A+B→A cached-result replay case. The scope identity currently uses `ConfirmationScopeId`, which is based on the wait episode. When a no-ID, same-fingerprint ambiguous wait narrows its candidates from A+B to B, the episode does not change; `ApprovalRevision` therefore stays the same and does not invalidate the old positive status. The real-pump probe demonstrates one wrong announcement in both causal and unordered modes. ANALYSIS-002 remains a release blocker.

No production settings, installed app, live approvals, audio or user sessions were touched. The orchestrator owns finding triage and task checkboxes.
