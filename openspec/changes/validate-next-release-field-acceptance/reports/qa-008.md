## Assessment and limitations

- The fix cycle holds under real interruption: a Ctrl+C in a separate console mid-scenario produced exit 130, a failing `run/interrupted`, a result file, no committed evidence and a clean machine, and the concurrency refusal and recovery work as documented. The one WM_CLOSE sample worked but remains best effort. Logoff and shutdown signals, a second Ctrl+C and a hung non-cooperative wait were not tested.
- The full `dotnet test` was not clean in my two runs (1 and 2 unrelated tray timing failures, all passing alone). This needs a decision from the orchestrator: re-run the suite on an idle machine, or accept as flaky. I do not report it as a finding because it is outside the changed scope and I cannot attribute it.
- One full harness run failed `isolation/production-unchanged` on a disappearing production process while another job on this machine was active; the immediately following run passed. This is the known accepted false-positive direction and I did not treat it as a new defect.
- Open and not touched by this recheck: ANALYSIS-124 (ANSI escapes in the evidence), the remaining provider-name branches (documented), the product options already decided by the user.
- No write outside this report and gitignored `artifacts/`. The ledger is not edited here.
