## Assessment and limitations

The revised trial meets the approved measurement definition: arithmetic mean of full-hook elapsed time minus arithmetic mean of the literal `echo 1` elapsed time through the same measured shell. Raw calculations match the summaries and stored gate flags. Both provider estimates are below 300 ms, raw native maxima remain below three seconds, and durable-count and crash-replay guards passed. The negative Codex estimate remains visible and is correctly described as measurement-relative rather than negative helper cost.

This conclusion applies to the revised baseline-adjusted metric only. It does not make the raw Codex end-to-end runtime less than 300 ms; the current raw Codex hook median is 479.954 ms, and the earlier 385.4529 ms raw-median failure remains a valid result under the old definition. No fresh benchmark was needed for this review because the raw per-sample data was retained and independently recomputed. No product suite, repackaging, installation, user configuration, or live provider trial was part of this bounded review.
